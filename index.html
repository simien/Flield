How to use Flield

Step 1 of 13

Generate, explore, export

Every composition is three colors, a background and two independent layers, built from a seed so it can always be reproduced. Explore variations with Random and Auto-randomize, then export a still image or a looping GIF.

General Layer A Layer B

Step 2 of 13

Three tabs, one canvas

General holds canvas settings; Layer A and Layer B hold each layer's own look.

Step 3 of 13

Switch between dark and light

The toggle switches dark and light, and your pick is remembered next time.

Web Tablet Tile

Step 4 of 13

Pick a size, or tile it

Web, Tablet, and Mobile presets set common dimensions. Tile squares the canvas and mirrors both layers so it repeats with no visible seam.

Step 5 of 13

Mask it into a circle or diamond

An optional shape mask clips the composition to a circle or diamond, with a seeded dithered edge so the boundary blends into the noise instead of cutting it off sharply.

Flow scale

Step 6 of 13

Each layer has its own settings

Layer A and Layer B each get their own seed, color, density, flow field, and symmetry, so they rarely match.

Density Range

Step 7 of 13

Narrow the range, keep the theme

Palette Range and Density Range constrain how far a randomized color or fill can wander, keeping Auto-randomize inside one tight, cohesive family.

Lock from randomization

Step 8 of 13

Lock a field to keep it

A locked field holds its value through a randomize pass instead of getting a new one. Lock All / Unlock All apply to everything at once.

Random

Step 9 of 13

Random rerolls what's unlocked

One click, and every unlocked field gets a fresh new value.

Step 10 of 13

Explore with Auto-randomize

Lock what you like, then slowly unlock the rest while it plays, watching results narrow toward a collection worth keeping. Prefer more control? Skip the button and click Random by hand instead.

Step 11 of 13

Undo steps back

Went too far? Undo walks back through recent passes, whether they came from a click, autoplay, or a GIF export.

Saved

Step 12 of 13

Save it, or share a link

Three save slots live in your browser; Copy Link encodes the same snapshot into a URL anyone can open.

Step 13 of 13

Export PNG, SVG, or GIF

The exported GIF replays the exact sequence Auto-randomize would generate.
